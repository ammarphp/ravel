# Supplied numerical and observer diagnostics

Use scientific specification version 2 and the ordinary `ravel plan --rundir <run> --spec <spec>` lifecycle for `likelihood-profile-probe`, `likelihood-candidate-check`, `likelihood-step-refinement`, `mpi-observer`, `mpi-paired-effect`, and `initialized-settings-retained`. Approval binds the selected input bytes, method decision, resource envelope and output destination. Native work remains behind the existing scientific admission and supervisor. Preparation reads inputs only; no adapter command grants launch authority.

Every input document requires a `plan` with `study_identity`, a finite `parameter_grid` (a list of named numerical parameter dictionaries), a unique `dataset_population` (supplied dataset identifiers), and `output: {format, file}`. The supported report formats are `json` and `jsonl`; `file` is a canonical relative path under the owned output directory. These fields describe the selected study and accompany its result. They supply no built-in model, event population, grid or reference answer. Missing plan fields raise `PlanError` with `code: missing_input` and the missing `field`.

## Numerical plans

`likelihood-profile-probe` derives parameter and observation dimensions from the pinned compiled model. The plan supplies `probe_selector`, `reference_selector`, distinct `review_actors` for the three required parity-review roles, `engine_sha256`, and `optimizer_maxiter`. The pinned retained input supplies every ordered start, bound, fixed coordinate, observation and feasible reference. `limits` supplies `adapter_seconds`, `per_backend_seconds`, `calls_per_backend`, and `witness_bytes`; `initial_escalation` declares each backend protocol. Failed, interrupted and unattempted starts remain in the denominator. A clean process exit, complete portfolio, independent objective, projected stationarity, finite-difference convergence, analytic-gradient agreement, feasible-reference guard and ending runtime custody remain separate checks.

`likelihood-candidate-check` launches no optimizer. Its plan supplies `candidate_selectors` as backend/index pairs, complete `trial_statuses`, `origin_properties` and exact `origin_commands`. It binds the originating scientific receipt, complete backend reports and source capsule. Its limits supply `adapter_seconds`, `numerical_seconds`, `numpy_and_fd_seconds`, `jax_seconds`, and `evaluator_calls`. The work census is derived from the actual vectors: each free interior coordinate uses four density calls, each active-bound coordinate six, plus independent/scalar/analytic values for each vector and reference. Arithmetic agreement, stationarity and reference quality are reported separately.

`likelihood-step-refinement` consumes the pinned candidate input, completed custody receipt and candidate report. Its plan supplies the complete `failed_pairs` of candidate/coordinate indices and `origin_properties`. Only convergence failures with retained analytic agreement and central finite-difference steps are admitted. Criteria supply two increasing `step_divisors`, the complete numerical tolerances, `coarse_to_quarter_drift_ratio`, and `fine_drift_at_most_coarse_fraction`. Work derives from selected candidates, pairs and divisors; limits supply `adapter_seconds`, `numerical_seconds`, and `evaluator_calls`. The evaluation journal preserves partial work and every original verdict.

All numerical tolerance objects supply positive `fit_requested`, `objective_agreement`, `stationarity`, `independent_density`, `gradient_atol`, `gradient_rtol`, and `step_scale`. Numerical agreement does not establish a global minimum, calibrated intervals, exclusion limits or physics validity.

## Observer and paired plans

`mpi-observer` requires a supplied topologically ordered `stages` graph. Each stage declares `id`, literal `argv`, owned relative `outputs`, `max_seconds`, and `depends_on`. Resources declare the original and selected populations, effect census, whole-stage deadline, one worker/attempt, retention ceiling, free-space floor, headroom, inherited bytes, lineage ceiling, and owned resource root. The plan declares `equivalence` (`identity_status`, `tagged_status`, `events`), `loader_stages`, and `generated_files`. Equality pairs, complete identity reports and tagged identity evidence remain required. Tagged transport helpers require `--events` and a supplied `--identities` JSON list; no replacement-ID roster is installed.

The whole-stage deadline must exceed a fixed 20-second custody reserve, and stage ceilings must total no more than the deadline less that reserve. Observer children, paired producers and retained verification stop at the reserve so comparisons, custody checks and failure records can still be written. Each `likelihood-profile-probe` backend keeps two seconds of its allowance to retain a failed report, and records an input refusal before any numerical work.

`mpi-paired-effect` uses supplied `stages`, `census`, `loader_stages`, `qualification_expectations`, `setting_contrasts`, and an `intervention` descriptor. Each setting contrast declares `group`, `field`, `on`, and `off`; expected initialized flags, modes, words and masses are supplied separately. The descriptor supplies `analyzed_events`, `original_events`, unique `original_ordinals`, `regions`, `target_region`, `trace_metadata`, `reference_gap`, `confidence_level`, `bootstrap_seed`, `bootstrap_replicates`, and `max_standard_error`. Its `arm_artifacts` maps both arms to supplied transport/settings/identity paths, expected producer command and complete producing stage IDs. The selected origin identity manifest uses `original_ordinal`; older external manifests need explicit conversion without changing event content.

The paired `signal_spec` supplies `channel_order`, a complete ordered `channel_map`, the complete allowed `trace_regions`, positive `cross_section_pb`, `kfactor`, and `luminosity_pb_inverse`. Each map row declares `channel`, `region`, `equals` (dotted retained-field equality predicates), and `lengths` (retained-list cardinality predicates). This preserves explicitly selected object/flavour conventions without installing a selection. The constant trace weight is cross section times correction divided by analyzed exposure; luminosity converts this to yields. The correction applies once. All paired events, including unselected events, contribute to covariance. Same-arm, cross-arm, and difference covariance retain observed channel overlap. Failed precision, zero bootstrap denominators or disagreeing interval classifications produce an inconclusive result. No favorable interval selection or automatic extension is permitted.

The ordinary admission binds the accepted initialized binary, its source/runtime bytes, current complete review journal, declared qualification population, cards, cohort, source predicates and complete clean producers. Installation, source agreement, synthetic tests and scientific qualification are distinct.

## Retained failed outputs

`initialized-settings-retained` launches no compiler, shower, fit or native child. Its task pins a new input document and digest. That document pins an `origin_contract` containing original resources, environment and stage graph, the failed report, supervised stage record, outer receipt, root quiescence, complete artifact manifest, and every selected source/stream/settings witness. The new resources are independently declared; the original contract remains immutable.

The plan's `failure_boundary` supplies `failure_classes`, ordered `phases`, and `execution_phase`. Recognized classes are `resource_floor`, `resource_ceiling`, `deadline`, `producer_exit`, and `custody_mismatch`. Report, stage and controller must agree on the terminal class; stage and execution-phase exit codes must agree and be nonzero. Completed cleanup and observed quiescence must cover the actual watchdog/supervisor/child lineage. Error-message wording and a particular exit-code number are not identity selectors. For immutable records without a typed class, the supplied `class_bindings` list binds `report`, `stage`, and `outer` classifications to each complete record's canonical SHA-256 digest. A changed or ambiguous binding refuses qualification; the original records remain untouched.

The plan also supplies `binary`, `transports`, `snapshots`, and `pairs`. Transport rows declare `id`, `folder`, pinned `source_lhe`, `events`, `encoding`, and the literal original producer `command`. Snapshot rows declare `id`, `file`, pinned `schema` and `compiled_source`, `command`, `settings`, and `masses`. Pair rows declare `id`, `disabled` and `enabled` stream maps, pinned `reference` streams, and `encoding`. All paths under the original output root must remain in the complete retained manifest. The verifier checks original producer receipts, recomputes transport from retained bytes, compares snapshots and observer pairs, validates loader custody and repeats input/output custody before successful completion.

The fresh free-space reserve includes the declared floor, remaining retention allowance and headroom. These are sampled checks, not an OS quota. Origin and new resource roots must be separate nonsymlink trees. The new result must not upgrade the original failed run, rewrite it or acquire new execution authority. A retained qualification covers only the selected bytes and witnesses.
