# Registered historical qualification helpers

The optional `helper_declaration` commits exact helper source, interpreter spelling
and binary, contract identity, phase roots and resource ceilings before planning.
The same declaration is carried through CHECK-IN 1, the stage fingerprint, launch
context and completion/reuse checks. Historical initial/final phases have distinct
roots beneath the selected attempt output. An admitted correction recomputes its
roots; it cannot inherit another attempt's destinations. Helper sources and the
interpreter are ordinary pinned inputs. An absent field preserves legacy identity.

The existing stage supervisor creates the owner before launching the worker.
Source-bound helper and anchor handshakes must complete before old-context work.
The owner observes their process generations and preserves cleanup authority after
worker death. Helper-enabled main-worker signals also require a fresh matching
generation. Unknown or reused identities require recovery. Cleanup shares one
bounded reserve, and an independent final census records remaining owned groups.
The normal metadata subtree ceiling is five processes, with one mathematical
worker. The 1 MiB owner registry is separate from the score worker's output budget.

This cooperative local protocol does not promise containment of hostile process
group escape, same-user tampering or arbitrary external SIGKILL. Kernel calls and
fsync are not preempted by Python deadline checks. Fresh qualified outer recovery
is required when custody cannot be established. A historical gate still requires
its exact old-source/runtime contract and a new receipt; old receipts and review
votes never authorize a changed scientific operation.

The `joint-score` scientific-study mode requires schema 2 and composes this owner
with the exact supplied source-law design and solver. Its four-fit/three-start
budgets and failure-retention contracts remain explicit. The current prospective
allocation is at most 300 seconds per fit, 1290 seconds internal work and 1350
seconds supervised work, with a separate helper cleanup reserve. These are ceilings,
not convergence guarantees. Numerical failures remain failures; no official fit is
requalified by source integration or synthetic transport controls.

Historical child diagnostics retain bounded append-only phase progress and an observed child poll result on closure failure. These records are ordinary pinned inputs when supplied in a receipt. They are observations, not admission or quiescence. The original metadata deadline, source gates and scientific checks remain mandatory. The preserved CMS timeout had no phase instrumentation and remains causally unresolved; this source change does not retry or reclassify it.
