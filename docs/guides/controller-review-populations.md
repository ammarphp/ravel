# Controller review populations

A review point connects a scientific duty to its required roles and evidence.
The approved controller plan retains the complete point policy. A check-in's
role roster identifies the expertise; its `seat_counts` identifies how many
independent occurrences that expertise requires. Two occurrences of one role
receive distinct sessions, output paths and checkpoint namespaces.

For example, a point requiring two derivations carries a count of two for its
deriver role. Both observed sessions must match the current plan, review round,
subject and point policy. The numerical duty also requires the pinned values,
their units and the declared comparison tolerance. Staffing evidence and the
numerical comparison are separate parts of the point's evidence contract.

An ordinary study's scope can select one compute rung and state its purpose.
That source-bound selection enters plan review before CHECK-IN 1. The child's
approval records the selected rung and exact prepared inputs. Runtime admission
checks the operator's frozen policy, resource ceilings and applicable GO against
those records. A revised scope releases its prior selection before renewed review.
