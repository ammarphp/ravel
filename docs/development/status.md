The v0.4.0 implementation covers typed limit transport, artifact-bound comparisons, explicit native normalization, capability dispatch, grounded draft intake, compact current-state handoff and durable dependency-aware execution. Independent review identified twelve cross-artifact consistency, approval, process-lifecycle, normalization, intent and claim issues. All twelve were repaired and independently rechecked. The final source suite has 1,089 passes, and the separate 40-test wheel run enables the two release-only cases.
