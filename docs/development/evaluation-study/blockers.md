continues around each one.

development and no longer block development ([decisions.md](decisions.md) E-34). Their rows stay

same direction authorized the 8-assignment engineering smoke, which ran on 2026-09-27
([smoke-record.md](smoke-record.md)); B-03 now holds what the pilot still needs.
