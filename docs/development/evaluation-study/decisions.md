# Evaluation study: decisions

Packet decision IDs are prefixed `PKT-` to avoid collision with the repository's D1–D18 failure
modes. Engineering decisions made by the integration lead are `E-xx`; they are reversible local
development choices, never scientific approvals. E-34 differs in kind: it records a direction the
budget owner gave on 2026-09-26 and how the integration lead applies it. **No question in section A
has been decided.** Under E-34 the human reviews are deferred to one consolidated review after
further development and no longer block development, and one 8-assignment engineering smoke is
authorized. Provisional defaults let development and that smoke proceed. No result produced under
them is reported as a scored or empirical finding until the deferred review has decided the
questions it depends on.

Review references: `R<lens>.<n>` (for example R4.0) is finding n of one lens of the holistic review
of `0fb3922`: lens 0 treatment identity, 1 accounting and custody, 2 documentation truth, 3 oracle
and scoring, 4 isolation. The "runner review" and "scoring review" are the reviews of the
`fix-runner` and `fix-scoring` repair tracks, their issues numbered in report order. These reports
are kept outside Git, in the ignored `local-runs/evaluation-slice/reviews/`:
`holistic-review-0fb3922.json` (`reviews[lens].findings[n]`, and `verdicts`, the skeptic pass over
the 14 major findings) and `repair-wave-reports.json` (each track's `fix:`, `review:` and `repair:`
report), and `integration-reports.json` (the integration workflow's hand-off, review, repair,
documentation and documentation-review reports; the integration review is `reports.rev`, and its
probe G is the `ipc_residue` issue).
